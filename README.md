# Will Henschell

Data scientist. I teach data analysis and SQL on [The Data Science Tutor](https://www.youtube.com/@TheDataScienceTutor).
I lead data services at a community bank, and through Greatest Ventures LLC I build small tools where
the math is tested and the assumptions are visible.

## Projects

- **[GreatestCalculators](https://greatestcalculators.com)** — free calculators for savings, loans,
  pay, seller fees and AI costs. Every formula is its own tested module, and the official
  marketplace-fee, AI-price and tax sources are checked automatically every morning.
- **[Greatest File Tools](https://greatestfiletools.com/)** — browser-based PDF, image, SVG and CSV
  tools. Your files are processed in your browser tab.
- **[Company Valuation Lab](https://greatestventuresllc.com/projects/company-valuation-lab)** —
  interactive valuation models built from public SEC filings.
